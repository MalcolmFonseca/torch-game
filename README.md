## About the Project
<p>
  This game was made for the 20 Second Game Jam 2025, where your game submission's playtime must be within 20 seconds. This was a collaborative effort between Malcolm Fonseca and Jacob Johnstone:
</p>
<a href="https://github.com/MalcolmFonseca/torch-game/graphs/contributors">
  <img src="https://contrib.rocks/image?repo=MalcolmFonseca/torch-game" alt="contrib.rocks image" />
</a>
<p>
  Knowing the inital time constraint for our game, along with the intrigue of adding the subtheme of light and shadow, we decided that the playable character could be made of fire,
  and the 20 seconds was simply how long it took for your flame to go out. This idea was perfect as it made the external constraints of the game jam fit the internal universe of the game we were designing.
</p>
<p>
  After coming up with the core idea of the game, we were still unsure of what the core game play loop should be. While we had many good directions to go with, we ended up settling on a fast-paced puzzle game,
  where along with the core puzzles to complete to survive, there was an extra puzzle spanning the entire game you could decipher to unlock a secret ending. This idea was heavily inspired by the puzzles and mystery behind the game Inscryption,
  which Malcolm finally played prior to the jam.
<p>
  The game's premise is as follows: After a troubling encounter with a wizard turning you into a sentient fireball, 
  you must solve puzzles and traverse obstacles to escape the caves you are trapped in. 
  A much larger puzzle must be solved to break the wizard's curse however, if you wish to return to your human form.
</p>
